Our Story

Born from a Love for the Sacred Himalayas

Tour and Travels King was founded with a single vision: to make the sacred and remote Himalayan destinations of Uttarakhand accessible to every devotee and adventurer – safely, comfortably, and meaningfully.

Our founder, Savita Kohali, grew up in the Kumaon hills and has spent over a decade personally guiding pilgrims to Adi Kailash, Om Parvat, Darma Valley, and the many lesser-known gems of the Pithoragarh district. Every package we offer has been personally walked, refined, and approved by our team.

Today, we are proud to have guided over 500 pilgrims and trekkers on transformative Himalayan journeys – and we carry each one of those experiences with us when we design new tours.
